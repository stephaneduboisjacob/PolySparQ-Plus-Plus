How a prediction is calculated · inputs · what the colors mean

Sources: Chainlink spot/opening reference (including supported settlement TWAP), completed Binance 1-minute candles, live Coinbase and Binance prices, Binance taker trades, fresh Polymarket UP/DOWN books, and asset-matched headline sentiment. Every card lists source ages, computed features and the signal adjustments used by the shadow model.

Color: green means UP is the current primary direction; red means DOWN; grey means the forecast is waiting for valid inputs. A bright lead/lag popup requires a fresh venue move, a fresh two-sided book, at least a 5 point model/market gap, and at least 2 points of stressed net edge after the configured fee and 0.5¢ slippage reserve. Those alerts remain experimental and are not a guarantee or an automatic order.
