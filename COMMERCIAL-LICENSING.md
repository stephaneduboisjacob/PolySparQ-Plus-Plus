## Contributions and commercial rights

The repository owner can license only rights they control. A contributor who submits code retains their copyright unless an agreement says otherwise. Contributions accepted under the project license do not automatically give the owner the right to relicense those contributions commercially. Before accepting third-party contributions for a future dual-licensing model, the project should adopt a lawyer-reviewed Contributor License Agreement (CLA) that explicitly grants the required relicensing rights. Until then, contributors should not assume their work will be included in a commercial license.

This document explains the project's intended licensing path; it is not legal advice. Have counsel review the license, contribution terms, and any commercial agreement before relying on them.
